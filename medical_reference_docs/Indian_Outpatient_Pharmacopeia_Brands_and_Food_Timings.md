# Indian Outpatient Pharmacopeia: Common Brand Names, Generic Molecules, Food Relations & Dispensing Rules

**Document Type:** Clinical Pharmacopeia & Dispensing Manual  
**Domain:** medicines  
**Region:** India (CDSCO / MoHFW / IPC Aligned)  

---

## 1. Respiratory, Allergy & Bronchodilator Formulations

### Montelukast Sodium + Fexofenadine Hydrochloride
* **Common Indian Brands:** Montair-FX, Telekast-F, Montek-LC, Allegra-M (Levocetirizine + Montelukast equivalent).
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Montelukast (10 mg) + Fexofenadine (120 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Allergic rhinitis, seasonal rhinorrhea, nasal congestion with allergic asthma symptoms.
* **Meal Relation & Administration:**
  * Take **once daily in the evening**. Can be taken with or without food.
  * Swallow whole with plain drinking water.
  * **Food Interaction Warning:** Do NOT take with fruit juices (grapefruit, apple, orange). Fruit juice polyphenols inhibit organic anion transporting polypeptides (OATP1A2), reducing fexofenadine bioavailability by up to 50%.
* **Safety & Side Effects:** Mild drowsiness, dry mouth, headache. Neuropsychiatric alert: Discontinue if abnormal vivid dreams, agitation, or depressive symptoms emerge.

---

### Levosalbutamol + Ipratropium Bromide (Respules / Nebulizer Inhalation Solution)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Duolin Respules, Duolin Inhaler, Combimist Respules.
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Levosalbutamol (1.25 mg) + Ipratropium Bromide (500 µg) per 2.5 mL unit dose.
* **Clinical Indications:** Acute bronchospasm, asthma exacerbation, Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) wheezing.
* **Administration Protocol:**
  * Inhalation via compressor jet nebulizer TDS (3 times daily) or QDS as prescribed.
  * **NOT for oral ingestion or drinking.**
  * Keep mouth and eyes protected. Accidental spray or leakage into eyes can precipitate acute angle-closure glaucoma or pupillary dilation.
* **Precautions:** Mild fine hand tremors, tachycardia, palpitations.

---

### Fluticasone Furoate / Fluticasone Propionate (Nasal Spray)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Flomist Nasal Spray, Furamist, Fluticone, Flohale.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Fluticasone Furoate (27.5 µg/spray) or Fluticasone Propionate (50 µg/spray).
* **Clinical Indications:** Allergic rhinitis, persistent nasal blockage, allergic nasal turbinate hypertrophy.
* **Administration Technique:**
  * Shake bottle well. Prime with 2-3 test sprays if unused for 7 days.
  * Blow nose gently before use.
  * Keep head upright. Insert nozzle into nostril pointing **slightly outward toward the ear** (away from the central nasal septum).
  * **Septal Warning:** Spraying directly onto the central nasal septum causes painful mucosal crusting, epistaxis (nosebleeds), and risk of septal perforation.

---

### Budesonide (Inhalation Respules / Dry Powder Inhaler)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Budecort Respules, Pulmicort.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Budesonide (0.5 mg / 1.0 mg per 2 mL).
* **Clinical Indications:** Maintenance anti-inflammatory therapy for bronchial asthma, acute croup in children.
* **Mandatory Counseling:**
  * **Rinse mouth thoroughly with water and spit out after every inhalation session.**
  * Failure to rinse mouth causes local immunosuppression leading to Oral Candidiasis (thrush) and hoarseness of voice (dysphonia).

## 2. Gastrointestinal & Acid-Peptic Formulations

### Pantoprazole Sodium + Domperidone
* **Common Indian Brands:** Pan-D, Pantocid-D, Pantodac-DSR, Pan-40 (plain Pantoprazole).
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Pantoprazole (40 mg) + Domperidone (30 mg sustained release).
* **Clinical Indications:** Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD), acid dyspepsia, reflux esophagitis with nausea.
* **Meal Relation & Administration:**
  * **STRICT DIRECTIVE: Must be taken on an empty stomach, 30 to 60 minutes before morning breakfast.**
  * Proton pump inhibitors (PPIs) require active acid secretion stimulated by post-fast food intake to covalently inhibit H+/K+-ATPase. Taking after breakfast reduces efficacy by >70%.
* **Long-Term Risks:** Prolonged unmonitored use (>1 year) increases risk of hypomagnesemia, Vitamin B12 deficiency, and osteoporosis.

---

### Rabeprazole + Domperidone / Esomeprazole
* **Common Indian Brands:** Razo-D, Rabekind-D, Nexpro-RD, Sompraz-D.
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Rabeprazole (20 mg) or Esomeprazole (40 mg) + Domperidone (30 mg).
* **Meal Relation:** Take once daily in the morning on an empty stomach 30-45 minutes before breakfast.

---

### Ondansetron
* **Common Indian Brands:** Emeset, Vomikind, Ondem.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Ondansetron HCl (4 mg / 8 mg tablet, syrup, or dispersible tab).
* **Clinical Indications:** Acute nausea and vomiting associated with gastroenteritis or chemotherapy.
* **Meal Relation:** Can be taken with or without food. In acute vomiting, administer 30 minutes before offering oral fluids or ORS.

---

### Oral Rehydration Salts (WHO Reduced Osmolarity Formulation)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Electral Powder, Walyte, Prolyte ORS.
* **Composition:** Sodium Chloride 2.6g, Glucose 13.5g, Potassium Chloride 1.5g, Trisodium Citrate 2.9g in 1 Liter (245 mOsm/L).
* **Preparation & Instructions:**
  * Mix entire sachet into **exactly 1.0 Liter of clean, boiled and cooled drinking water**.
  * Sip frequently after every loose motion or bout of vomiting.
  * **Do NOT boil the reconstituted solution.** Discard any unused solution after 24 hours. Do NOT add extra sugar or salt.

## 3. Antimicrobial & Antibacterial Formulations

### Amoxicillin + Potassium Clavulanate (Co-Amoxiclav)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Augmentin 625, Clavam 625, Moxikind-CV 625, Advent 625.
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Amoxicillin (500 mg) + Clavulanic Acid (125 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Acute bacterial sinusitis, otitis media, community-acquired pneumonia, skin/soft tissue infections.
* **Meal Relation & Administration:**
  * **Take at the START of a meal or immediately with food.**
  * Food enhances clavulanate absorption and drastically reduces gastrointestinal cramping, nausea, and diarrhea.
* **Mandatory Directive:** Complete the entire prescribed duration (typically 5 to 7 days) even if symptoms completely resolve by day 3 to prevent antibiotic resistance.

---

### Azithromycin
* **Common Indian Brands:** Azithral 500, Azee 500, Zithrox 500.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Azithromycin Dihydrate (500 mg tablet).
* **Clinical Indications:** Streptococcal pharyngitis/tonsillitis, atypical respiratory infections, bronchitis.
* **Meal Relation & Administration:**
  * Standard: Take once daily. Tablets may be taken with food if gastric distress occurs.
  * Standard adult course is strictly **once daily for 3 to 5 days**.
* **Cardiac Safety Alert:** Macrolides can cause QT interval prolongation. Use with caution in patients with cardiac conduction disorders or hypokalemia.

---

### Cefixime / Cefpodoxime Proxetil
* **Common Indian Brands:** Zifi 200, Taxim-O 200, Cefolac 200 (Cefixime); Gudcef 200, Monocef-O 200, Doxcef 200 (Cefpodoxime).
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Cefixime (200 mg) or Cefpodoxime Proxetil (200 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Acute uncomplicated urinary tract infection (UTI), typhoid fever, bacterial bronchitis.
* **Meal Relation:** Cefpodoxime should be taken **with food** to maximize gastrointestinal absorption. Cefixime can be taken with or after meals.

---

### Ciprofloxacin / Levofloxacin
* **Common Indian Brands:** Ciplox 500, Cifran 500 (Ciprofloxacin); Levomac 500, L-Cin 500 (Levofloxacin).
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Ciprofloxacin (500 mg) or Levofloxacin (500 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Complicated UTIs, acute pyelonephritis, severe infectious diarrhea, typhoid fever.
* **Food & Chelation Warning:**
  * **Take with plenty of fluids.**
  * **Do NOT take concurrently with antacids (magnesium/aluminium), calcium tablets, milk/dairy products, or iron supplements.** Multivalent cations chelate fluoroquinolones in the bowel, cutting absorption by up to 90%. Separate by at least 2 hours.
* **Black-Box Alert:** Risk of tendinitis and Achilles tendon rupture. Discontinue at first sign of calf or tendon pain.

---

### Metronidazole
* **Common Indian Brands:** Flagyl 400, Metrogyl 400.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Metronidazole (400 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Amoebic dysentery, giardiasis, dental abscesses, anaerobic pelvic infections.
* **Alcohol Prohibition Directive:**
  * **Strict zero-alcohol rule during treatment and for 48 hours post-treatment.**
  * Ingesting alcohol triggers a violent disulfiram-like reaction (severe throbbing headache, flushing, nausea, projectile vomiting, and tachycardia).

---

## 4. Analgesics, Antipyretics & Anti-Inflammatory (NSAIDs)

### Paracetamol (Acetaminophen)
* **Common Indian Brands:** Dolo 650, Calpol 500/650, Crocin 650, Pacimol 650.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Paracetamol (500 mg / 650 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Pyrexia (fever), mild to moderate headache, body ache, sore throat pain.
* **Dosing & Ceiling Limits:**
  * Adults: 500 mg to 650 mg every 4–6 hours as needed (SOS).
  * **CRITICAL UPPER LIMIT: Maximum daily adult ceiling is 4,000 mg (4 grams) in 24 hours.**
  * Exceeding 4 grams per day exhausts glutathione stores and causes toxic NAPQI accumulation, resulting in fulminant acute hepatic failure. Antidote is N-Acetylcysteine (NAC).

---

### Aceclofenac + Paracetamol / Diclofenac
* **Common Indian Brands:** Zerodol-P, Hifenac-P, Voveran 50 (plain Diclofenac).
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Aceclofenac (100 mg) + Paracetamol (325 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Acute musculo-skeletal pain, joint inflammation, dental pain, trauma swelling.
* **Meal Relation & Gastric Protection:**
  * **STRICTLY take after meals (PC) with a full glass of water.** Never take on an empty stomach.
  * In patients with a history of gastritis or acid reflux, co-prescribe with a PPI (Pantoprazole) to prevent NSAID-induced gastric mucosal erosion.

---

### Mefenamic Acid + Dicyclomine
* **Common Indian Brands:** Meftal-Spas, Cyclopam.
* **Active Generic Molecules:** Mefenamic Acid (250 mg) + Dicyclomine HCl (10 mg or 20 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Spasmodic dysmenorrhea (period cramps), acute intestinal colic spasms.
* **Meal Relation:** Take with or after food to minimize gastric irritation.

---

## 5. Metabolic & Cardiovascular Outpatient Formulations

### Metformin Hydrochloride
* **Common Indian Brands:** Glycomet 500/850/1000, Glyciphage, Cetapin.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Metformin HCl (500 mg, 850 mg, 1000 mg extended release).
* **Clinical Indications:** Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (first-line oral therapy).
* **Meal Relation:**
  * **Take with or immediately after meals (breakfast and dinner).**
  * Taking with food significantly reduces gastrointestinal side effects (nausea, metallic taste, diarrhea, abdominal cramps).
* **Safety Alert:** Contraindicated in severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min) due to risk of lactic acidosis.

---

### Amlodipine Besylate
* **Common Indian Brands:** Amlong 5, Stamlo 5, Amlopin 5.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Amlodipine (5 mg / 10 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Essential hypertension, chronic stable angina.
* **Administration & Monitoring:** Take once daily, typically in the morning. Common benign side effect: Bilateral dependent pedal edema (ankle swelling), which is vascular rather than cardiac/renal failure.

---

### Telmisartan
* **Common Indian Brands:** Telma 40, Telmikind 40, Tazloc 40.
* **Active Generic Molecule:** Telmisartan (40 mg / 80 mg).
* **Clinical Indications:** Essential hypertension, cardiovascular risk reduction in diabetics.
* **Administration & Monitoring:** Take once daily with water. Check baseline serum creatinine and potassium periodically. Absolute contraindication in pregnancy (fetotoxic).
