## 1. Pediatric Fever Care & Safe Home Management

### How to Sponge a Feverish Child Correctly
* **The Lukewarm Water Rule:** Always use **lukewarm tap water (around room temperature, 30°C - 32°C)**.
* **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN:**
  * **NEVER use cold water, ice water, or ice packs.**
  * **NEVER use alcohol or spirit rubs.**
  * *Why?* Extreme cold causes sudden constriction of blood vessels in the skin, which traps heat inside the vital organs and triggers violent shivering. Shivering causes muscle metabolism that spikes the internal core body temperature higher, increasing the risk of febrile seizures.
* **Technique:** Dip a soft washcloth into lukewarm water, squeeze out excess moisture, and gently wipe the child's forehead, neck, armpits, and groin (where major superficial blood vessels lie). Continue for 10 to 15 minutes in a well-ventilated room with a gentle fan. Dress the child in light cotton clothes; do not bundle them in heavy blankets.

### Paracetamol Pediatric Dosing
* **Standard Pediatric Dose:** **10 to 15 mg per kg of body weight** per dose, given every 4 to 6 hours as needed (maximum 4 doses in 24 hours).
* Always check the concentration on the syrup bottle:
  * Regular infant drops: usually 100 mg / 1 mL.
  * Standard pediatric suspension: usually 120 mg or 125 mg / 5 mL.
  * Forte pediatric suspension: usually 250 mg / 5 mL.
* Always use a calibrated measuring syringe or spoon, not a domestic tea spoon.

### What to Do During a Febrile Seizure (Convulsion)
* Febrile seizures can occur in young children (6 months to 5 years) when body temperature spikes rapidly.
* **Immediate Actions:**
  1. Gently place the child on their side in the **recovery position** on the floor to keep the airway clear of saliva or vomit.
  2. Clear away any sharp objects or furniture.
  3. **DO NOT put anything in the child's mouth** (never insert spoons, cloth, fingers, or water). Children will not swallow their tongue, but forcing objects can break teeth or obstruct the trachea.
  4. Time the seizure. Most febrile seizures stop spontaneously within 2 to 3 minutes.
  5. Once the jerking stops, take the child immediately to the nearest pediatrician or emergency clinic.

## 2. Diarrhea, Vomiting & Oral Rehydration Therapy (ORT)

### The WHO ORS Preparation Standard
* **How to Prepare:** Dissolve the **ENTIRE packet of WHO-formula ORS into EXACTLY 1 Liter of clean drinking water** (boiled and cooled). Mix thoroughly until completely dissolved.
* **Critical Safety Rules:**
  * Do NOT guess the water amount or mix half-packets with half a glass. Making ORS too concentrated causes hypernatremic osmotic diarrhea, pulling more water out of the body into the gut.
  * Do NOT add sugar, honey, juice, or milk to the ORS solution.
  * Discard any unused prepared ORS solution after 24 hours and prepare a fresh batch.
  * Drink in small, frequent sips (especially if nauseous or vomiting).

### Zinc Supplementation in Diarrhea
* In children with acute diarrhea, taking **Zinc supplements (20 mg daily for children > 6 months, 10 mg daily for infants < 6 months) for 14 consecutive days** regenerates the damaged intestinal mucosal lining, reduces the duration and severity of the current episode, and protects against recurrent diarrhea for the next 3 months.

### Danger Signs of Severe Dehydration (Immediate Hospitalization Needed)
* Sunken eyes with no tears produced when crying.
* Severely dry, parched mouth and tongue.
* Inability to drink or vomiting everything taken orally.
* No urination for more than 6 to 8 hours.
* Extreme lethargy, drowsiness, or difficulty waking up.

## 3. First Aid for Animal Bites (Dogs, Cats, Monkeys, Bats)

### The 15-Minute Running Water Rule
* **The Single Most Important Action:** Immediately wash the bite or scratch under **running tap water with soap for AT LEAST 15 FULL MINUTES**.
* *Clinical Reason:* The rabies virus has a lipid-containing outer membrane. Thorough washing with standard household alkaline soap dissolves this lipid envelope, mechanically washing away over 90% of the viral particles before they can enter the peripheral nerve endings.

### STRICTLY FORBIDDEN in Animal Bites
* **NEVER apply turmeric powder, lime, chili powder, mud, herbal pastes, or kerosene.** These cause caustic chemical burns, trigger local tissue necrosis, and dramatically increase the risk of rabies entry and secondary bacterial infection.
* **DO NOT bandage or suture the wound tightly.** Rabies is an anaerobic neurotropic virus. Leaving the wound open allows drainage.

### Post-Exposure Rabies Prophylaxis Schedule
* Visit an anti-rabies clinic or emergency hospital immediately.
* **Category II Bites (Scratches without bleeding):** Anti-Rabies Vaccine (ARV) on **Day 0, Day 3, Day 7, and Day 28** + Tetanus Toxoid.
* **Category III Bites (Any bite or scratch that breaks skin with bleeding, or lick on broken skin/mucosa):** Requires both the full vaccine course **PLUS Rabies Immunoglobulin (RIG)** infiltrated directly into and around the wound on Day 0.

## 4. First Aid for Burns

### Initial Treatment
* **Cool Immediately with Clean Running Water (10 to 20 minutes):** Gently pour cool tap water over the burn to draw heat out of the skin and halt deep thermal tissue destruction.
* **STRICTLY PROHIBITED:**
  * **NEVER apply ice or ice water.** Extreme cold on burned skin causes vasoconstriction and frostbite on already compromised tissue (*ischemic necrosis*).
  * **NEVER apply toothpaste, raw egg, butter, or flour.** These trap heat, harbor dangerous bacteria, and cause severe wound infection that doctors must painfully scrub away.
* **Blister Care:** Never pop or puncture burn blisters; the blister skin is a natural sterile biological bandage.
* Cover loosely with sterile gauze or clean plastic wrap.

## 5. Critical Emergency Red Flags: Go to the Hospital (ER) Immediately

### Cardiovascular Emergencies (Heart Attack / Acute Coronary Syndrome)
* Sudden heavy, squeezing, or crushing pain in the center of the chest.
* Pain radiating to the left shoulder, left arm, neck, jaw, or upper back.
* Accompanied by unexplained cold sweating, shortness of breath, dizziness, or vomiting.

### Neurological Emergencies (Acute Stroke - FAST Test)
* **F - Face Drooping:** One side of the face droops or is numb. Ask the person to smile; is the smile uneven?
* **A - Arm Weakness:** One arm is weak or numb. Ask the person to raise both arms; does one arm drift downward?
* **S - Speech Difficulty:** Slurred speech, unable to speak, or difficulty repeating a simple sentence.
* **T - Time to Call Emergency:** Every minute counts; rapid thrombolytic treatment within 4.5 hours can prevent permanent brain paralysis.

### Severe Infections & Dengue Warning Signs
* **Meningitis:** High fever accompanied by a stiff neck (inability to touch chin to chest), severe throbbing headache, and intense light sensitivity (photophobia).
* **Dengue Critical Phase Warning Signs (Typically when fever starts subsiding around Day 4-6):**
  * Severe, unrelenting abdominal pain.
  * Persistent vomiting (cannot keep liquids down).
  * Bleeding from gums, nose, or blood in vomit/stools (black tarry stools).
  * Sudden extreme fatigue, cold clammy hands, restlessness, or sudden drop in urine output.
